This entry is one true memory, kept small on purpose: at three in the morning, on the night a relationship ended, a person eats cold nasi goreng straight from the pan with a spoon, standing at the kitchen sink. No restaurant, no celebration — just sweet soy sauce and a fried egg, and the surprise that something that day finally did not hurt. The program writes that single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. What makes the meal the best is not the food itself — it is the moment the food stops being fuel and becomes the first gentle thing in a hard day.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Entry

> At 3 a.m. after she left, I ate cold nasi goreng from the pan with a spoon, standing at the sink, and the sweet soy and fried egg were the first thing that day that did not hurt.
